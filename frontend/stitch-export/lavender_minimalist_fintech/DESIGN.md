## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-clean, serene, and prestigious fintech environment engineered specifically for modern Indian investors, earners, and digital-first households. It strips away traditional corporate aggression and heavy bank saturation in favor of a soothing, pristine visual sanctuary rooted in modern white minimalism with restrained soft lavender accents.

The design movement embodies **Soft Minimalist Modernism**:
- **Clarity & Air:** Pristine white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed by whisper-light lavender boundaries (`#EAE7F2`) create an airy, unencumbered canvas where cognitive fatigue is minimized.
- **Intelligent Elegance:** Soft violet accents deliver high-end sophistication, distinguishing the platform from legacy institutional blues while sustaining resolute credibility and security.
- **Data Precision:** Every data element is tuned for clarity. Tabular numerals, localized Indian currency syntax (₹ Lakhs & Crores), and restrained semantic feedback loops ensure fast, error-free financial comprehension.

## Colors

The color palette centers on pure whites, gentle violet tints, and deep violet-slate typography. It completely replaces heavy royal blues and bold mustard yellows with a controlled, calm financial hierarchy.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#8B5CF6`):** Soft violet utilized deliberately for primary button fills, active tab indicators, selected interactive states, and core progress milestones.
- **Primary Hover / Active (`#7C3AED`):** Deepened violet for micro-interactions, active press states, and primary button hover feedback.
- **Secondary (`#F5F3FF`):** Light lavender wash deployed for badge backgrounds, subtle callout containers, and selected table row highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#FAF9FF`):** Whisper lavender used as an ultra-soft card hover backdrop and nested container tint.
- **Neutral / Text Primary (`#1E1B2E`):** A high-density, deep violet-tinted slate engineered for contrast, legibility, and high-trust authority across headers and ledger figures.

### Surfaces & Boundaries
- **Main Canvas:** Pure `#FFFFFF` and crisp secondary off-white `#F8FAFC` establish a clean, uncluttered foundation.
- **Cards & Modules:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with distinct hairline borders.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#EAE7F2`):** Soft lavender-tinted neutral borders that separate data modules without imposing heavy visual cages.

### Semantic Accents
- **Income / Positive (`#16A34A`):** Pure green for inflows, SIP capital appreciation, asset growth, and positive budget deltas.
- **Expenses / Negative (`#E05252`):** Balanced crimson for debits, bill outflows, and portfolio pullbacks.
- **Warnings & Alerts (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber restricted strictly to time-sensitive bill notices, pending KYC approvals, or compliance reminders.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on the mechanical discipline of **Inter**, using proportional hierarchy and OpenType numerical features to deliver clarity.

### Numerical Standards & Currency Formatting
- **Tabular Figures:** All financial tables, metric cards, and currency inputs must enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to guarantee aligned columns and uniform width for digits.
- **Indian Numbering Localization:** Amounts conform to the Indian Lakhs/Crores numbering standard (e.g., `₹12,48,500.00` and `₹1.42 Cr`).
- **Currency Glyph:** The `₹` symbol sits flush against the numeric digits without whitespace. The symbol matches the font-weight of the adjacent number.
- **Hierarchy:** Primary financial totals leverage `metric-xl` with high contrast (`#1E1B2E`), while supporting dates, labels, and metadata remain in `label-sm` or `caption` utilizing muted slate (`#64748B`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a 4px/8px modular rhythm, prioritizing balance and data density across responsive breakpoints.

### Structural Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fluid 12-column layout capped at 1440px maximum content container, paired with a 250px navigation bar. Columns maintain `gutter-desktop` (24px) with canvas margins of `margin-desktop` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Navigation condenses to a slim 72px rail or header trigger. Layout uses `gutter` (16px) and canvas margin of `margin-tablet` (24px).
- **Mobile (<767px):** Single-column stacked stream with an app-style bottom navigation bar. Margins are locked to `margin` (16px), and interactive touch targets maintain a minimum dimension of 44px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, diffused shadows, transparent tints, and hairline borders, avoiding heavy skeuomorphic shading.

- **Level 0 (Flat Baseline):** Canvas backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) and resting input fields.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** Resting state for summary modules and tables. Constructed with a hairline border (`1px solid #EAE7F2`) and an ambient, low-opacity drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.03), 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Interactive Hovers):** Applied to active popovers, filter menus, and hovered card cards. Uses `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.03)` with a primary-tinted border (`1px solid #8B5CF6`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Used for transfer modals, goal-setting dialogs, and slide-in trays. Combines `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.04)` with a frosted backdrop overlay (`rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.3)` accompanied by a `backdrop-blur-sm`).

## Shapes

The design uses a clean, disciplined shape language that keeps technical financial charts grounded while remaining smooth and modern.

- **Base Radii (8px / `rounded`):** Default for interactive controls, including primary/secondary buttons, text input fields, selector chips, and search bars.
- **Container Radii (12px / `rounded-lg`):** Summary metrics cards, account breakdown containers, transaction tables, and analytics modules.
- **Modal Radii (16px / `rounded-xl`):** Dialog overlays, drawer frames, and confirmation cards.
- **Pill Radii (Full Rounded / `9999px`):** Status indicator tags (e.g., "Auto-Pay Active"), UPI transaction identifiers, category chips, and percentage fluctuation badges (`+8.4%`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#8B5CF6` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 500`, border-radius 8px. Hover shifts to `#7C3AED`. Focus ring: `focus:ring-2 focus:ring-[#8B5CF6]/30 focus:ring-offset-2`.
- **Secondary / Soft Lavender:** `#F5F3FF` background, `#8B5CF6` text, hairline border `1px solid #EAE7F2`. Hover shifts to `#FAF9FF` with `#7C3AED` text.
- **Outline / Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #EAE7F2` border and `#1E1B2E` text. Hover shifts to `#FAF9FF` surface.
- **Destructive:** Soft crimson background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FECACA`, text `#E05252`. Hover darkens to `#E05252` fill with white text.

### KPI & Financial Summary Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #EAE7F2` border, 12px corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Padding: 20px (`space-xl`).
- Top row: Metric title in `label-sm` (`#64748B`) paired with a soft lavender icon bubble (32x32px, 8px rounded, `#F5F3FF` background, `#8B5CF6` icon fill).
- Metric row: Total value in `metric-xl` (`#1E1B2E`) formatted with Indian comma placement (e.g., `₹24,50,000`).
- Bottom row: Comparison tag using rounded pills: positive growth takes `#F0FDF4` background with `#16A34A` text (`+12.4% vs last mo`), and outflows or deficits take `#FEF2F2` background with `#E05252` text.

### Form Inputs & Monetary Fields
- Height 42px, radius 8px (`rounded`), resting background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE7F2`, text `#1E1B2E`.
- Left-addon affix: Dedicated `₹` currency glyph in `#8B5CF6` with a right border divider.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#8B5CF6` with a soft lavender outer glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`).
- Formatting: Automated comma structuring conforming to Indian numbering as characters are typed (e.g., `5,00,000`).

### Transaction Ledger Table
- Header: Track height 40px, uppercase `label-sm` in `#64748B` over an alternating `#FAF9FF` background.
- Row: Track height 56px, bordered bottom with `1px solid #EAE7F2`. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#F5F3FF`.
- Ledger Cells:
  - Party/Merchant: Displayed with a 36px circular avatar or bank icon.
  - Payment Tag: Mini pill for rails like `UPI`, `NEFT`, `IMPS`, or `NACH` styled with `#F5F3FF` background and `#8B5CF6` text.
  - Value Column: Formatted in tabular numerals. Inflows carry `#16A34A` with a `+₹` prefix; debits carry `#1E1B2E` with a `-₹` prefix.

### Data Visualizations & Charts
- **Area Line Charts:** 2px stroke using `#8B5CF6`, paired with an underlying linear gradient fading from `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.18)` at the curve down to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.0)` at the axis. Tooltip adopts pure white surface, `1px solid #EAE7F2`, and tabular metrics.
- **Spend Breakdown Ring:** High-clarity donut segments composed of Soft Violet (`#8B5CF6`), Emerald (`#16A34A`), Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`), Slate Blue (`#64748B`), and Rose (`#E05252`). The center void shows the net monthly expenditure.

### Alerts & Notifications
- **Alert Banners:** Bordered container with an amber accent marker (`3px solid #F59E0B`), soft background `#FFFBEB`, and `#1E1B2E` copy for imminent loan EMIs, credit card statement cuts, and advance tax dates.
- **Success Toasts:** Minimalist white card with a 3px `#16A34A` left border, green check icon, and `#1E1B2E` status description.
